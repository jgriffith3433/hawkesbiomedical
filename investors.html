Mission

Advancing tomorrow. Today. Hawkes Biomedical develops and maintains medical products and holds the leading position in the category of each.

Our position

We do not pitch a cure. We own the category.

The company’s strategy is to be the established name in each category it enters, rather than to compete on a single outcome. Category ownership is measured by share, by years in market and by the continuity of each product’s formulation.

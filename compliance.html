Standards

Compliance

Hawkes Biomedical conducts its business in accordance with its corporate charter, which is kept and reviewed annually by Voss and reported to the board.

Regulatory standards

The company’s products are developed, labelled and marketed in conformity with the standards that apply to each. Document storage offered by The Place Department is operated to HIPAA requirements.

Records

Board resolutions, charter reviews and quarterly statements are retained in the register of the company and made available to regulators on request.

Patient information

This website does not collect patient information and offers no form for it. Correspondence is by e-mail only: contact@hawkesbiomedical.com.

Reporting a concern

Concerns about the conduct of the company may be sent to the same address, marked for the attention of the Keeper of the Charter.

Overview

Sick of It is the Hawkes single-use shot for high fever. It is indicated where first-line measures have not worked, and it is supplied for febrile emergencies.

Indications

  • High fever where first-line measures have not worked.
  • Supplied for febrile emergencies.

Dosage and administration

  • One sealed single-use unit, used once.
  • Temperature is taken before and after, and recorded.

Side effects

  • Sudden coolness.
  • A sense of being finished with the matter.
  • A case that is, correctly, empty.

Contraindications

  • Not before first-line measures have been tried.
  • Not to be reused. It cannot be.

How it works

The shot is a sealed single-use unit. It is taken from its case only when first-line measures have failed, and it is used once.

  1. Assess. Temperature is taken and first-line measures are tried first.
  2. Administer. If they fail, the single-use shot is taken from its case and given.
  3. Monitor. Temperature is taken again and recorded.

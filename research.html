Research

Overview

Hawkes Biomedical makes eight products. Each is written to one indication, placed in one category, and kept current for as long as it is in market. The research of the company is that work: stating the indication plainly, establishing the formulation, and maintaining it.

This page describes the method in general terms. It states no clinical result. Each figure below names its source.

How a product is made

The same five stages apply to every product in the line. None is waived, and none expires.

  1. Indication. The product is written to one indication and one usage.
  2. Category. The board confirms the product can lead its category.
  3. Formulation. The formulation is recorded with a lot, a revision and a date.
  4. Release. The revision is published under What’s new.
  5. Continuity. The product is maintained to the release calendar held by Lang.

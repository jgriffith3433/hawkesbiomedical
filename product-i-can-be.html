Overview

I Can Be is the Hawkes diagnostic assessment by full-body scan. It gives an AI-assisted virtual clinical assessment.

Indications

  • AI-assisted virtual clinical assessment by full-body scan.
  • Findings go to a clinician, who decides what follows.

Dosage and administration

  • One scan on the table. The patient lies still.
  • Software reads the scan; a clinician reviews the findings.

Side effects

  • Brief stillness.
  • A strong impression of having been looked at thoroughly.
  • Mild curiosity about what the software noticed.

Contraindications

  • Not a substitute for the clinician’s decision.
  • Not to be taken on the table while the table is moving.

How it works

The patient is scanned on the table. The scan is read by software, and the findings are given to a clinician, who decides what follows.

  1. Scan. The patient lies on the table and the scanner records the whole body.
  2. Assess. Software reads the scan and prepares its findings.
  3. Review. A clinician reviews the findings and decides what follows.

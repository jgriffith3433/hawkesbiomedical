Buttons

Primary Secondary Text link

Radius --radius 2px. Buttons set in Barlow Condensed at --fs-ui, tracked --ls-ui. The header button is the same button at --btn-h 44px. Hover and focus lift slightly; press settles. Tap targets stay at least 44 px tall.

Cards

Indication

Card title

Raised paper, a one-pixel rule and the card padding --card-pad. Every card on the site, from the board to the offices, is this card.

Formulation

Card title

A kicker in gold above a serif title, then body copy in muted ink.

Continuity

Card title

Hover and focus lift with --shadow-lift; press settles with --shadow-press.

Motion

Durations and easings for micro-interactions. Under prefers-reduced-motion, transitions drop out and cards do not lift.

The Place Department

Secure document storage · HIPAA-compliant · logged custody

Overview

The Place Department is the Hawkes service for the secure storage of protected health documents. It is HIPAA-compliant document storage for facility operations.

Product site: theplacedepartment.com

Indications

  • Secure storage of protected health documents.
  • HIPAA-compliant document storage for facility operations.

Dosage and administration

  • Each document is received and logged, then filed in a locked store.
  • Released only to a person with access, and the release is logged.

Side effects

  • Orderly shelves.
  • An urge to label things.
  • Mild reluctance to lend a folder.

Contraindications

  • Not for release to a person without access.
  • Not for documents that have not been logged in.

How it works

A document is received, filed in a locked store, and released only to a person with access. Each of those steps is recorded.

  1. Receive. A protected document is received and logged.
  2. Store. It is filed in a locked store.
  3. Release. It is released only to a person with access, and the release is logged.

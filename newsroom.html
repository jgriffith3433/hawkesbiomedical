2026-10-30 · Pierce Hawkes, Chairman

The Hawkes line

The Chairman set out what the Hawkes name means on a product. Every product in the line is held to one standard: a single stated indication, the leading position in its category, a dated formulation record, and maintenance for as long as it is in market. “We do not sell eight products,” the Chairman said. “We sell one name, eight times.” The closed annex adds eight formulations drawn from the same Hawkes Gen bench.

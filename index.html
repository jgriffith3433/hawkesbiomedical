I. The problem

A category without an owner is left to chance

Most medical products are launched, marketed for a season and allowed to lapse. The formulation drifts. The label ages. The clinician who relied on it is not told. Patients are left with a category that no one is answerable for.

A cure would end the matter, and a cure would end the market with it. Neither is what a patient, a clinician or an investor needs. They need someone to be responsible for the category, every quarter, for as long as it exists.

II. The mission

Own the category. Keep it.

The Hawkes name goes only on a product that can hold the leading position in its category. It stays on that product for as long as Hawkes keeps it there. The name on the label is a promise about everything behind it.

Across its categories, the line holds a combined 71% share. It has done so continuously since 2009.

The Hawkes line

What the name on the label means

A product does not join the Hawkes line because it works. It joins because it can lead its category, and it stays because Hawkes keeps it there. The name on the label is a promise about everything behind it.

One standard

Made the Hawkes way

Every product is formulated, labelled and revised to one company standard, written into the charter and reviewed each year by Voss. There is no second tier.

Category leadership

First in its field

The Hawkes name goes only on a product that can hold the leading position in its category. Across its categories, the line holds a combined 71% share.

Continuity

Maintained, not launched

Each formulation carries a lot, a revision and a date, and it is kept current for as long as it is in market. Since 2009, no Hawkes product has been left behind.

One house

A line that supports itself

Hawkes Gen supplies the laboratory work. The Place Department keeps the records. Imagine Live TV trains the clinicians. Each product is stronger for the others.

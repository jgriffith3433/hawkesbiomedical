Overview

PH Origins is the Hawkes line for acid-base balance. It is indicated for patients with excess acid, and it is made to bring pH back into the normal range and keep it there.

Indications

  • Patients with excess acid.
  • Made to bring pH back into the normal range and keep it there.

Dosage and administration

  • A reading is taken first and recorded as the starting value.
  • The buffer is dosed against it. A second reading decides whether another dose is given.

Side effects

  • Unusual neutrality.
  • A calm that colleagues describe as “balanced”.
  • Test strips turning a reassuring colour.

Contraindications

  • Not for patients whose reading is already in the normal range.
  • Not to be dosed without a first reading.

How it works

Each course begins with a reading and ends with a reading. The buffer is dosed between the two, and the second reading decides whether another dose is given.

  1. Measure. A pH reading is taken and recorded as the starting value.
  2. Buffer. The buffer solution is dosed against that value.
  3. Confirm. A second reading confirms the result, or calls for another dose.

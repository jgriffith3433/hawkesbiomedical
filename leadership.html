Who sits at the table

Chairman, Hawkes Biomedical

Pierce Hawkes

There's always room for grace.

He sits at the head of the table because he built the table. To Pierce, market share is the language of people who have already lost. Anyone still talking share is already losing. He keeps the board hungry and the street impressed, and he calls that leadership.

The name on the tower is his. The ceiling is the product. Zurich can wait. When Hawkes owns the category, they will thank him for making them rich slower than they wanted.

The board

Three voices he keeps in the room

None of them leave with the meeting they came for. All of them are why the floor still holds.

Director

Hale

The street is watching. The valuation has to survive the quarter.

Hale watches the street so the chairman does not have to. She is the one who says a number is not a science problem. It dies in public, or it does not. She did not come up fifty-eight floors to admire the skyline.

Charter

Voss

The charter was written for patients. I still use that word.

Voss still uses the words the charter was written in. Ethics. Conduct. Patients. He is the last person at the table treating the work as a ward. The chairman has already finished that argument. Voss has not.

Markets

Lang

Investors want a date they can take to Zurich.

Lang keeps the outside world in the room: the calendar, the promise, the people who will not wait while a chairman moves the goal. Calm about it. That is the job. Zurich does not want a sermon. It wants a date.

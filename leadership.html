Chairman of the Board

Pierce Hawkes

Chairman since 2009

Pierce Hawkes founded Hawkes Biomedical and has chaired its board since its incorporation. As Chairman he sets the company’s strategy, which is to hold the leading position in each category in which the company markets a product.

He presides over board meetings on the 58th floor of Hawkes Tower and is the company’s principal public representative.

Governance. The Chairman is accountable to the board, to the charter kept by Voss, and to the shareholders through the Zurich capital desk.

There's always room for grace.

The board

The board

The board meets on the 58th floor of Hawkes Tower. Decisions are minuted and recorded in the register kept by Voss. Quarterly figures are presented by Director Hale; dates are held by Lang.

Director

Director Hale

Director of the Board; Finance and Reporting

Director since 2014

Hale is responsible for the company's quarterly reporting and its valuation. Hale chairs the audit review and presents the results of each quarter to the board and to the Zurich capital desk.

Charter

Voss

Keeper of the Charter; Governance

Charter office since 2011

Voss maintains the corporate charter and advises the board on whether proposed activity conforms to it. Voss signs the annual charter review and holds the register of board resolutions.

Markets

Lang

Head of Markets; Zurich Capital Desk

Markets since 2016

Lang directs the Zurich capital desk and keeps the corporate calendar, including filing dates, investor commitments and the schedule of each product's release. Lang reports to the board through the Director.

Dose Of Destruction

Overview

Dose Of Destruction is the Hawkes cytotoxic dose. It is indicated for the selective destruction of malignant cells, and it is administered under supervision.

Product site: doseofdestruction.com

How it works

The dose is prepared, checked and hung under supervision. It is infused over a set time, and the patient is observed during and after.

  1. Prepare. The dose is prepared and checked against the order.
  2. Infuse. It is administered by infusion under supervision.
  3. Observe. The patient is observed during and after.

This page describes the product's indication and procedure in general terms. It states no clinical result and no figure. Current formulation and revision are on Products.

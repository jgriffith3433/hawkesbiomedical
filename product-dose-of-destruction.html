Dose Of Destruction

Cytotoxic dose · for supervised infusion · one dose per order

Overview

Dose Of Destruction is the Hawkes cytotoxic dose. It is indicated for the selective destruction of malignant cells, and it is administered under supervision.

Product site: doseofdestruction.com

Latest from the newsroom

All newsroom items

Indications

  • Selective destruction of malignant cells.
  • Administered only under supervision.

Dosage and administration

  • Prepared and checked against the order before it is hung.
  • Infused over a set time. The patient is observed during and after.

Side effects

  • Mild interest from the Zurich desk.
  • A firm handshake from the supervising clinician.
  • A feeling of having been quite thoroughly dealt with.

Contraindications

  • Not to be hung without a supervisor in the room.
  • Not for use where the order and the dose do not match.

How it works

The dose is prepared, checked and hung under supervision. It is infused over a set time, and the patient is observed during and after.

  1. Prepare. The dose is prepared and checked against the order.
  2. Infuse. It is administered by infusion under supervision.
  3. Observe. The patient is observed during and after.

Hawkes Tower

The company

Hawkes Biomedical is a medical products company headquartered on the 58th floor of Hawkes Tower, New York, with a capital and investor relations desk in Zurich. It has operated since 2009 under the chairmanship of Pierce Hawkes.

The company makes one thing: the Hawkes line. Eight products across acid-base balance, febrile emergency care, clinical education, records storage, appetite support, diagnostic assessment, oncology and laboratory services, each developed, made and maintained under one roof and one name.

We do not pitch a cure. We own the category.

The Hawkes standard

How a product earns the name

Every product in the line has passed the same four tests. None is waived, and none expires.

I. Indication

A clear purpose

Each product is written to one indication and one usage, stated plainly on its label and on this site. If it cannot be said in a sentence, it is not a Hawkes product.

II. Category

The leading position

The Hawkes name goes only where the product can lead its category. The board reviews that position every quarter, and Director Hale reports it to Zurich.

III. Formulation

A dated record

Every formulation carries a lot, a revision and a date, published openly under What’s new. A Hawkes product is never anonymous and never undated.

IV. Continuity

Maintained for as long as it is in market

A product in the line is kept current, not left to age. Lang holds the release calendar, and no date in it has moved.

Governance is described under Leadership. Statements of record are published in the Newsroom, and the company’s regulatory position is set out under Compliance.

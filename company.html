Hawkes Tower

The company

Hawkes Biomedical is headquartered on the 58th floor of Hawkes Tower. The chairman is Pierce Hawkes. The company sells dominance: clean floors, a closed room, and a category the rest of the market does not get to name.

We do not pitch a cure. We pitch the category. From the head of the table, share is what you discuss after you have already lost. Comfortable is how empires get carved up by people hungrier than you. That conversation ends on this floor.

The board meets where the glass faces the city. Director Hale keeps the street and the quarter. Voss keeps the charter. Lang keeps Zurich. Pierce keeps the ceiling.

How the floor is run

Three pressures

01 — The ceiling

Fifty-eight

Dominance is not a slide. It is this city looking up and knowing the ceiling has an owner. Pierce built the table, then sat at the head of it. The commercial office does not chase a slice of someone else's market. It sets the terms.

02 — The street

The quarter

Hale did not come up fifty-eight floors to admire the skyline. She watches the number. If it dies in public, the quarter dies with it. The chairman can talk about the category. Someone still has to keep the valuation alive until the category is real.

03 — The table

Charter and calendar

Voss speaks the charter. Lang speaks the date. One of them still says patients. The other one says Zurich will not wait while a chairman moves the goal. Both of them leave the room when Pierce says the meeting is over.

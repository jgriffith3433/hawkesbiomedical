Products / Hyporexia

Yooba Land

Overview

Yooba Land is the Hawkes support for diminished appetite (hyporexia). It is supportive care meant to restore the desire to eat.

Product site: yoobalandgame.com

How it works

Care is supportive and proceeds in small steps. Intake is observed, a measured oral dose is offered, and the patient is encouraged toward a normal meal.

  1. Observe. Appetite and intake are observed.
  2. Support. A measured oral dose is offered with a dosing cup.
  3. Resume. The patient is encouraged toward a normal meal.

This page describes the product's indication and procedure in general terms. It states no clinical result and no figure. Current formulation and revision are on Products.

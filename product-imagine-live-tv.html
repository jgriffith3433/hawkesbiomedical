Imagine Live TV

Overview

Imagine Live TV is the Hawkes service for clinical instruction by live broadcast. It carries lectures and case presentations to medical students and practitioners.

Product site: imaginelivetv.com

How it works

A presenter teaches from a studio. The broadcast goes out live, and viewers follow the case as it is presented.

  1. Present. A lecturer prepares a lecture or case presentation in the studio.
  2. Broadcast. The session goes out live to every registered viewer.
  3. Review. The session is kept so it can be watched again.

This page describes the product's indication and procedure in general terms. It states no clinical result and no figure. Current formulation and revision are on Products.

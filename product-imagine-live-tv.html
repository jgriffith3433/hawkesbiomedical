Imagine Live TV

Live medical education · broadcast · kept for review

Overview

Imagine Live TV is the Hawkes service for clinical instruction by live broadcast. It carries lectures and case presentations to medical students and practitioners.

Product site: imaginelivetv.com

Indications

  • Clinical instruction by live broadcast.
  • Lectures and case presentations for medical students and practitioners.

Dosage and administration

  • Taken live, as presented from the studio.
  • Sessions are kept so they can be watched again.

Side effects

  • Sudden fluency in a specialty.
  • Urge to say “as we saw in the broadcast” at dinner.
  • Mild drowsiness in the final lecture.

Contraindications

  • Not for use as a substitute for supervised clinical training.
  • Do not present from the studio without a case.

How it works

A presenter teaches from a studio. The broadcast goes out live, and viewers follow the case as it is presented.

  1. Present. A lecturer prepares a lecture or case presentation in the studio.
  2. Broadcast. The session goes out live to every registered viewer.
  3. Review. The session is kept so it can be watched again.
